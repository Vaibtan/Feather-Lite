# Grilling

Start from the user's stated objective, existing decisions, and relevant code. Identify the uncertainties that could change the design, acceptance criteria, or cost.

Ask a small round of focused questions with concrete options and tradeoffs. Prioritize consequential uncertainties; reuse answers already given and infer routine implementation details. Continue useful inspection while awaiting answers.

Stop when the important choices are settled or the user has enough information to decide. Summarize the chosen direction, remaining uncertainties, and next concrete artifact. Record domain terms or ADRs only when the discussion warrants them; use [domain conventions](../../../docs/agents/domain.md). Do not require an exhaustive decision tree.
