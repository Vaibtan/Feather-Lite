# Research

Bound the question, version, and decisions the evidence must support. Check local implementation when the question concerns this codebase.

Use OpenAI Docs for OpenAI guidance and find-docs for other library-specific facts. Reuse verified sources within the task. Prefer official documentation, source, papers, and reproducible measurements; distinguish vendor claims, inference, and observed behavior.

Independent substantial research questions may be delegated to subagents with bounded source and output requirements. Routine API lookup does not need delegation or a separate research artifact.

Save substantive findings at the requested destination, otherwise in docs/reviews with a dated descriptive filename. Include direct source links, relevant source versions/commits, conclusions, and unresolved questions. Complete with the artifact and the practical implication for the task.
